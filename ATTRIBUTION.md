# Bits and Bots — source attribution

This file records the origin and licensing of educational materials used in the Bits and Bots curriculum. It is intended to accompany the source files and generated PDFs, HTML presentations and other distributed materials.

## Bits and Bots original materials

Original Bits and Bots lesson plans, worksheets, challenge cards and slide content are intended to be published under the [Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 4.0 International licence (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), unless a file states otherwise.

**Branding exception:** The Bits and Bots name, robot logo and other identifying brand artwork are excluded from the content licence. See [BRANDING.md](BRANDING.md). Third-party content remains subject to its own licence and attribution requirements.

## Micro:bit Educational Foundation

The Bits and Bots curriculum uses BBC micro:bit hardware and may refer to teaching ideas and activities published by the Micro:bit Educational Foundation.

The Foundation identifies relevant educational content as published under **Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)**. The licence is available at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/.

When a lesson is actually adapted from a Foundation resource, record:

- The original lesson or resource title and direct URL.
- The author or rights holder, as credited by the source.
- The source licence and a link to it.
- What was changed (for example, rewritten instructions, new exercises or a different age range).
- Any source-specific credit, image restrictions or exceptions.

Example attribution **template** (replace placeholders before publishing):

> Adapted from “[Original resource title]” by the Micro:bit Educational Foundation ([source URL]), licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Changes: [describe changes]. The adaptation is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.
