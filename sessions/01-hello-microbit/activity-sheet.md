**Mission:** Make your micro:bit show a message you choose.

## Make it

1. Open **MakeCode** at <https://makecode.microbit.org/> and start a **New Project**.
2. Find the **Basic** blocks and drag **show string** inside **on start**.
3. Change the text to your initials, nickname or a fun word.
4. Watch the simulator. Does your message scroll?
5. With your leader's help, download the program to your micro:bit.
